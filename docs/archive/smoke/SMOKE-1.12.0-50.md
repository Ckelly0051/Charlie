# GridIron IQ 1.12.0-50 - Coach Smoke

## Defensive reporting

1. Open a tagged season and choose **Reports -> Defense**.
2. Confirm **Full season** is selected and Defensive Snaps reflects all of our
   defensive games, not opponent-scout film.
3. Compare **All Runs**, **All Passes**, **Run Inside**, **Run Outside**, and the
   available pass categories. Check one known row against film.
4. Click a play-type row. The first matching clip should open; Next should move
   through only that row's plays, including games across the season.
5. Switch to **Current game**. Confirm Defensive Snaps and the tables narrow to
   the open game. Switch back to Full season.
6. In **Best Calls by Opponent Play Type**, open one Front, Coverage, and Blitz
   Decision answer. Confirm the film matches both the opponent play type and
   the named defensive answer.
7. Review **Game Trend** and **Situational Defense** for plausible values.
8. Confirm **Scheme Detail** and **Defensive Self-Scout** still render below the
   new performance sections.

Metric definitions:
- Stop Rate: inverse of offensive Success Rate using the existing down/distance
  rule.
- Explosive allowed: run 12+ yards; pass 16+ yards.
- Red Zone TD Rate: red-zone possessions ending in a touchdown, not touchdown
  snaps divided by red-zone snaps.
- Havoc: sack, interception, fumble, or qualifying negative play.
