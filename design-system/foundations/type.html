Foundations · Type

Field numerals,
not scoreboard clichés

Every sports product reaches for condensed type. But the numbers actually painted on a football field are wide — heavy, flat-sided, built to be read from the top row. Going wide where everyone else goes narrow is the cheapest distinctiveness available to us, and it is more authentic, not less.

The move

ArchivoOmnibus Type · OFL · variable weight + widthRecommended

One variable family covers everything: Expanded 800 for field numerals, regular width 400–600 for UI text, narrow where space is tight. Sturdy grotesque with real character and none of the neutral-startup feel of Inter or Geist. A single variable file is also smaller than the two static Barlow weights we ship today.
Bricolage GrotesqueOFL · variable · quirky by design
41 · 04th & 2
More personality and genuinely memorable. Risk: its quirks get tiring across 60 rows of dense tagging data, and it fights the film for attention.
AntonOFL · single weight · poster display
41 · 04th & 2
Maximum impact for headlines and the wordmark. One weight only, so it cannot carry a system — viable as an accent face over Archivo, not as the display voice.
Barlow Condensedalready bundled today · 58.7 KB, weights 600/700
41 · 04th & 2
The incumbent. Perfectly competent and completely expected — this is the face that makes the app read as a tidy sports dashboard rather than as something with a point of view.
