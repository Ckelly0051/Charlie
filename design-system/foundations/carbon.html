Carbon gives us the system.
It must not give us the brand.

Carbon is the best-considered open system for dense, complex data products, and it has a genuinely well-tuned dark theme. It is also unmistakably IBM. Taken wholesale it produces exactly the enterprise-corporate look we are trying to avoid — so we take its structure and refuse its identity.

This is a systematic answer to the exact problem in our build today — every surface sits at the same mid-grey, so nothing recedes and nothing leads. Carbon solves it with tested tokens instead of hand-picked hexes. We extend it downward with one surface Carbon has no need for: film, darker than the app shell, so video is always the brightest object on screen.

Where I push back: take the model, not the greys

Carbon's ramp — pure neutral

Zero cast, zero warmth, zero temperature. It is tested, and it is also the exact quality that makes every Carbon product look like the same admin console. Adopting these values verbatim would make us more generic, not less.

Same steps, cool cast

Identical step rhythm and contrast ratios, tuned blue-cool — a night game under lights. We keep everything Carbon proved about structure and discard the part that is only IBM's taste.

The blue matters more than it looks

IBM Blue $interactive#0f62fe — reads as IBM
Line of scrimmage#2b6fff — same job, our meaning
First down#ffd400 — Carbon has no equivalent

Nearly the same hue, deliberately not the same value. We keep Carbon's role for interactive blue and attach football's meaning to it, then add a first-down yellow Carbon has no concept of. That single addition is most of what stops this reading as an IBM product.

The type fork — your call

A · All-Plex, Carbon-faithful
4TH & 2

Plex Sans Condensed for numerals, Plex Sans for UI, Plex Mono for data. One family, maximum coherence, and it inherits Carbon's craft. Cost: condensed is the category default, and it is closer to IBM's own look.

B · Plex for interface, wide for the field
4TH & 2

Plex Sans + Mono carry the interface; a wide face carries football numerals only. Keeps Carbon's craft where the density lives and keeps a distinct voice where the identity lives — the numbers.

The objection worth taking seriously: Carbon is an enterprise admin design system. The complaint about this app is that it feels like a re-skinned utility — and leaning hard on Carbon is a plausible way to make that worse, not better. Carbon can make Reports genuinely excellent, because a scout report IS a dense data product. It cannot make the app feel like football. Nothing in Carbon knows what a first down is.

So the split has to be deliberate: Carbon governs the data surfaces — Reports, Study, Plan, Settings, the Film Room grid — where its craft is a direct upgrade over what we have. Identity comes from the three things Carbon has no opinion about: the film, the field, and the numerals. If we let Carbon govern those too, we will have built a very well-organised admin console for football.
